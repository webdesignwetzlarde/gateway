# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-25

## Context

Kopist is intended to outlive its founder's involvement and to move to a neutral foundation. Future
maintainers, reviewers and auditors need to know why the design looks the way it does.

## Decision

Significant decisions are recorded as architecture decision records in `docs/adr/`, following the
MADR style: context, decision, consequences. Records are immutable once accepted; changes are made by
superseding records.

## Consequences

- Design discussions end in a written, linkable decision.
- Every ADR is licensed CC-BY-4.0 like the rest of `docs/`.
